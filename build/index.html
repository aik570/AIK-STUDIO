07QuestionsBefore you ask

Good questions.

How much of my time does it take?

One to three calls of 30 minutes. We prepare everything else and show you results, not homework.

What does it cost?

A landing page is 900–1 400 €, a website 2 400–4 600 €, larger projects more. You get a fixed price after the first call.

Who owns the website?

You do. Domain, hosting, code and all access are handed over at launch.

How does payment work?

We sign a short contract. 50 % before we start, 50 % at launch.

What about Impressum, Datenschutz and DSGVO?

Legal pages and a privacy-friendly setup are part of the build. For legal advice we recommend your Steuerberater or lawyer.

What happens after launch?

Care by subscription (150–300 € per month) or by the hour — or you run it yourself.

Do you work in German and English?

Yes. Websites in German, English or both.
